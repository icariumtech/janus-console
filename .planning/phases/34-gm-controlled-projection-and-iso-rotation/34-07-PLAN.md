<objective>
Close UAT gap G-34-6 (minor, test 6): selecting a location with an authored default_view flashes the previous map in the wrong view before the correct map appears, on the GM console only.

Purpose: root cause (debug session .planning/debug/34-default-view-flicker.md, confirmed): GM EncounterView hands MapPreview an orientation and locationKey taken immediately from the new activeView, but the map and the hull come from state that is not cleared on a location change and is refetched by two independent, unguarded effects. The orientation cut therefore runs against the PREVIOUS location's map, then a mixed frame (new rooms with old hull, or the reverse) is painted, then the correct map. The player terminal is unaffected (map, slug and view arrive in one payload). Fix: bind map, manifest and the view they are rendered under as one unit, load the two requests together, keep the old unit frozen in its own view until the new one is complete, and guard against out-of-order responses.

Output: a useLocationMapBundle hook, GM EncounterView wired to it, a vitest regression test, and the codemap updated. No server, player-side, MapPreview or renderer change.
</objective>
